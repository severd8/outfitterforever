# Outfitter Forever

**Outfitter, ported to World of Warcraft: Forever.**

Outfitter Forever is an equipment manager: save your gear as outfits and switch between them with one click, or let them change by themselves while you ride, fish, swim, enter a city, change stance and more.

It works like the Outfitter you know, with the same outfits, menus and scripts. It's based on [Outfitter (Retrofit)](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/outfitter-retrofit) by GovtGeek, which continues [Outfitter](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/outfitter) by John Stephen (Mundocani).

## Features

- **Outfits** for any set of gear, including the ranged and ammo slots: complete outfits, and accessories that go on top (fishing pole, riding gear, ...).
- **Automatic outfits** with ready-made scripts, or your own.
- **Quick access** from the character window, the minimap button, an outfit bar, key bindings, macros (`/outfitter`) and data broker addons.
- **Copy outfits** from your other characters.
- **Easy to read**: set the window's size, fonts and colors.
- **Fitted to Forever**: its classic gear and classes, its combat rules, and the same flat look as the other Forever addons.
- **In your language**: English, German, French, Chinese, Korean and Russian.

Each file on the Files tab lists what's new in that version.

## Installation

Install it with the CurseForge app, or download the file and unzip it into your WoW: Forever `Interface\AddOns` folder. Then restart the game or type `/reload`. Don't install another copy of Outfitter alongside it.
